# 3D Adaptation F1 Figure

**Suggested dissertation caption:** Mean held-out CART F1 by target attack family and adaptation budget. Each value averages the eight models trained on the other attack families. The surface connects discrete attack-family categories only to support visual comparison; it does not imply a continuous relationship between attack families.
