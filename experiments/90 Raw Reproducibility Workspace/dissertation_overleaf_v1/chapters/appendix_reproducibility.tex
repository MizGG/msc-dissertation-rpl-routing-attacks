\chapter{Reproducibility and Evidence Map}
\label{app:reproducibility}

The primary frozen dataset is generated by \texttt{experiments/final\_simulated\_dataset\_v1/build\_dataset.py}. Its audit is produced by \texttt{audit\_dataset.py}. The dataset contains 810 windows and 90 complete family, mode, and seed groups. The audit verifies timing, labels, completeness, uniqueness, and marker exclusions.

The principal result sources are the cross-attack matrix, whole-seed adaptation summary, online detector comparison, robustness adaptation summary, and sinkhole defence condition summary. All figures in the dissertation are generated from these preserved CSV files. Raw attack markers are used only to verify activation and are excluded from IDS and drift inputs.
